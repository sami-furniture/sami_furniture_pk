From Raw Timber To Your Living Room.

Trace the lifecycle of a Sami Furniture House commission. Every piece passes through five distinct rites of master craftsmanship before entering your sanctuary.

Atelier Process Gujranwala Workshop
01
Stage 01

Seasoned Timber Selection

We exclusively harvest kiln-dried, seasoned Sheesham (Rosewood) and Teak. By rigorously testing moisture content below 10%, we eliminate warping, cracking, or seasonal expansion in Pakistan’s changing weather.

02
Stage 02

Architectural Joinery

Master woodworkers cut mortise-and-tenon joints, interlocking timber pieces mechanically rather than relying solely on nails. Heavy load points are reinforced to endure decades of daily living.

03
Stage 03

Artisanal Hand Finishing

Surfaces are sanded through five progressive grits of paper, followed by multiple coats of hand-rubbed natural oils or Italian polyurethane lacquers to celebrate the wood's deep organic grain.

04
Stage 04

Luxury Upholstery

High-density imported HR foam, reinforced webbing, and hand-selected velvet, chenille, or top-grain leather are tailored seamlessly. Every seam is double-stitched for structural permanence.

05
Stage 05

Installed in Your Sanctuary

White-glove delivery across Pakistan. Our team handles transit, uncrating, and precision placement inside your home so your space is immediately transformed.
